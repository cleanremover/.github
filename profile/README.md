## CleanRemover

[CleanRemover](https://cleanremover.com) is an online AI photo and video cleanup tool, best known for removing watermarks. It uses AI inpainting to rebuild what sits behind watermarks, logos, text, emojis and unwanted objects, instead of blurring or cropping.

### Tools

| Tool | What it does |
| ---- | ------------ |
| [Watermark Remover](https://cleanremover.com) | Remove watermarks and logos from photos |
| [Video Watermark Remover](https://cleanremover.com/remove-watermark-from-video) | Remove watermarks from MP4, MOV and WebM clips |
| [Gemini Watermark Remover](https://cleanremover.com/gemini-watermark-remover) | Runs on your device; the image is never uploaded |
| [Object Remover](https://cleanremover.com/remove-object-from-image) | Remove people and objects from photos |
| [Text Remover](https://cleanremover.com/remove-text-from-image) | Remove captions, dates and other text |
| [Emoji Remover](https://cleanremover.com/remove-emoji-from-photo) | Remove emoji stickers from photos |
| [Cleanup Pictures](https://cleanremover.com/cleanup-pictures) | Brush over anything you want gone |

Photo tools are free to try without signup. Video watermark removal uses one-time credit packs.

CleanRemover is meant for content you own or have the right to edit.
